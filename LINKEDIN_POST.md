## LinkedIn post draft

I’m excited to share my Power BI portfolio project: **Customer Segmentation & Retention Analytics**.

Using a synthetic customer dataset, I prepared customer-level data, created DAX measures, and designed a dashboard concept to explore:
- Customer and revenue KPIs
- Customer segments and loyalty tiers
- Retained, at-risk, and churned customer distribution
- Revenue by segment and purchase channel
- Interactive filters for region, channel, segment, and tier

This project helped me practice Power Query, data modeling concepts, DAX, KPI design, and business-focused dashboard storytelling.

The dataset is fictional and the dashboard is an educational portfolio demonstration, not a production churn-prediction model.
